Requirement: the look reaches modern quality — detailed ship models, current effects (explosions, trails, lighting), rich textures, no visible mesh popping or flat facets — and the field of view gives a readable scale (an opening that looks wide enough must be wide enough for the ship).
**Why:** there is no second chance for a first impression; visual quality is a first-class goal, not polish for later.
**How to apply:** treat visual quality and readable scale as a priority track next to milestone features; every wave that touches the client raises the look; judge with screenshots sent to the maintainer ([[maintainer-phone-review]]). See [[next-steps]].
