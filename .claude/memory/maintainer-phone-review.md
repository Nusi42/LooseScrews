Give the maintainer documents to review (plans, reports) as a published, commentable HTML page, not paths to local .md files. Send screenshots of the running game to the maintainer's phone regularly: at least after every milestone, ideally after each integrated wave (for example `target/browser-smoke/*.png` from the browser smoke test).
**Why:** the maintainer follows the project mostly from the phone and cannot open local files there.
**How to apply:** after writing a plan or report for review, publish it and give the link; after a green wave or milestone, send fresh screenshots. See [[next-steps]], [[orchestration-preferences]].
